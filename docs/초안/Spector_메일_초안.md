## 메일 본문 (영문)

**Subject:** Korean translation of the WLCS and a question about subscale scoring — doctoral dissertation on firefighters

Dear Professor Spector,

My name is [이름], and I am a doctoral candidate at [소속 대학원] in South Korea. I am writing to notify you of a Korean translation of the Work Locus of Control Scale, as requested in your conditions for use, and to ask for your guidance on one scoring question.

**1. Korean translation (attached)**

I have translated the 16 WLCS items into Korean for my dissertation research. The attached document includes the copyright notice (© 1988, Paul E. Spector), my name as translator, and the year. The scale is used for non-commercial doctoral research only, and no fee is charged to participants.

**2. Study context**

My dissertation examines whether an internal locus of control is *always* advantageous for safety in high-risk, highly interdependent teams. The sample is Korean firefighters (fire suppression and emergency medical personnel). I model work locus of control as a predictor of feedback-seeking behavior, which in turn predicts safety citizenship behavior, with perceived error management climate as a moderator.

**3. My question — separate internal and external subscale scores**

My design treats internality and externality as **two separate variables** rather than a single summed score. I am aware this departs from the scoring you specify, in which the eight internal items are reverse-scored and all 16 items are summed so that high scores indicate externality.

My reasons for considering the two-factor approach are:

- Macan, Trusty, and Trimble (1996) reported evidence for a two-factor structure and recommended computing the subscales separately.
- Oliver, Jose, and Brough (2006) found that a two-factor model still fit poorly and that a three-factor solution fit best, which suggests the internal items may not be unidimensional.
- Your own cross-cultural work (Spector, Sanchez, Siu, Salgado, & Ma, 2004) suggests that control beliefs operate differently in East Asian contexts, where secondary control is more salient. This is central to my argument that externality should not be equated with helplessness.

I would be very grateful for your view on two points:

1. Whether you consider separate internal and external subscale scores defensible, and under what conditions.
2. Whether you are aware of any existing Korean-language validation of the WLCS. I have not been able to locate one, and if none exists I plan to report a confirmatory factor analysis comparing one-, two-, and three-factor models in my sample.

I also plan to use a 5-point response format rather than the original 6-point format, in order to match the other scales in my questionnaire. I would welcome any caution you have about this, particularly regarding the absence of a neutral midpoint in the original design.

Thank you very much for making the scale freely available for research, and for any guidance you are able to offer.

With respect and gratitude,

[이름]
[학위과정 / 전공]
[소속 대학원]

## 왜 이 메일이 중요한가

1. **의무 이행** — 번역 시 사본 송부는 척도 사용 조건이다. 안 보내면 조건 위반이다.
2. **최대 쟁점의 방어 자료** — 내적·외적 분리 채점은 이 논문 심사의 최대 표적이다.
   개발자 본인의 회신만큼 강력한 방어 자료는 없다.
3. **불리한 근거를 먼저 꺼내는 전략** — Oliver et al.(2006)의 불리한 결과를 메일에서
   먼저 언급했다. 이는 심사에서도 같은 태도를 취하기 위한 것이다.
   반박당하기 전에 스스로 한계를 제시하면 논증의 신뢰도가 올라간다.
4. **비용 0원, 소요 10분.** 회신이 없어도 "송부하였다"는 기록 자체가 조건 이행 증거다.
